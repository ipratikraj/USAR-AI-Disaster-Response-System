---

### 2. Ekdum Aasan Hinglish Samjhaav & Desi Analogy
Dekho dosto, disaster management mein ek term hoti hai jise bolte hain **"The 72-Hour Golden Window"**. Iska simple matlab yeh hai ki agar kisi insaan ko malbe (rubble) ke neeche se pehle 24 ghante ke andar nikal liya jaye, toh uske zinda bachne ke chances **80% se zyada** hote hain. Lekin jaise-jaise time beetta hai—pyaas, asphyxiation (saans na aana), internal bleeding aur hypothermia (thand lagna) ki wajah se—72 ghante khatam hote-hote survival rate **20% se bhi kam** ho jata hai!

Ab reality samjho:
Jab bhookamp aata hai, toh pehle 3 se 6 ghante tak bahar se koi rescue team nahi pahunch paati. Sadkein tooti hoti hain, traffic jam hota hai, aur rescue machinery transport karne mein time lagta hai. Is 3 se 6 ghante ke dauran wahan sirf society ke resident security guards aur aam log hote hain. Is waqt ko kehte hain **"Operational Vacuum"** (yaani zero official help).

Agar humare paas aam phone ka camera ya normal RGB camera ho, toh woh dhool aur andhere mein kuch nahi dekh sakta—screen par sirf kaala andhera ya safed dhund dikhegi. Humara system thermal radiation use karta hai, jo dhool ke partikals ke paar nikal kar insaan ki body heat detect kar leta hai!

---

### 3. Core Engineering Rationale
* **Biological Survival Curve Equation:**
  $$P_{\text{survival}}(t) = P_0 \cdot e^{-\lambda t}$$
  Yahan survival probability $t$ (time) ke saath exponentially girti hai. Decay constant $\lambda$ uninjured victim ke mukable crush injury wale victim ke liye 4x zyada fast hota hai.
* **Optical vs. Thermal Penetration:**
  Visible light ki wavelength bohot chhoti hoti hai ($0.4 - 0.7\ \mu\text{m}$), isliye hawa mein tairti hui dhool (smoke/dust particle size $\approx 1 - 5\ \mu\text{m}$) visible light ko chitar-bitar (scatter) kar deti hai. Isse normal camera blind ho jata hai. Thermal Infrared ki wavelength lambi hoti hai ($8 - 14\ \mu\text{m}$), jo in particles ke beech se bina takraye seedha camera sensor tak pahunch jaati hai.

---

### 4. Technical Terms - Aasan Bhasha Mein
* **Operational Vacuum:** Disaster aane ke baad ka woh initial waqt (3-6 ghante) jab sarkari rescue agencies field tak nahi pahunch paati hain aur local survivors ko khud hi rescue karna padta hai.
* **RGB Camera:** Normal camera jo Red, Green aur Blue visible light capture karta hai (jo insaan ki aankhein dekh sakti hain). Andhere ya dhool mein yeh 100% fail ho jata hai.
* **Zero-Lux Environment:** Aisa andhera jahan roshni ka ek bhi photon na ho (absolute total darkness). Thermal camera ko roshni ki zaroorat hi nahi hoti kyunki woh heat radiation capture karta hai.

### 5. Advanced Viva Voce Questions & Answers

#### Q1: "Can you explain the mathematical relationship between victim survival probability and time under rubble?"
* **Model English Answer:**
  > "Empirical epidemiological disaster studies demonstrate that survivor extraction adheres to an exponential decay formulation: $P(t) = P_0 \cdot e^{-\lambda t}$. In blunt trauma and partial asphyxiation scenarios, $\lambda$ accelerates sharply after 24 hours. While probability of extraction survival is approximately 80% to 90% within the first 12 to 24 hours, it decays monotonically below 20% by hour 72 due to acute compartment syndrome, dehydration, and hemorrhagic shock. Our edge system targets the immediate 0 to 6-hour window to maximize survival before that steep descent."
* **Hinglish Defense:** *"Sir, victim ka survival exponential decay follow karta hai. 24 ghante ke baad zinda bachne ke chances rapid tareeke se girte hain. Humara system pehle 6 ghante mein hi survivors pinpoint karta hai."*

#### Q2: "Why can't high-intensity LED flashlights resolve the visibility problem for standard RGB cameras?"
* **Model English Answer:**
  > "High-intensity LEDs worsen the problem due to Rayleigh and Mie back-scattering. When strong optical light hits dense particulate matter (calcium carbonate dust and suspended concrete smoke), the light scatters backward directly into the camera lens, creating blinding glare. In contrast, Long-Wave Infrared (8–14 µm) wavelengths are significantly larger than smoke particles, permitting direct unattenuated transmission."

## Chapter 04: Thermal Infrared Physics & Atmospheric Penetration (Slide 04)

### 1. Slide Metadata & Visual Identity
* **Slide Category:** Physical Sciences & Sensor Electromagnetics
* **Official Title:** Thermal Infrared Physics & Atmospheric Penetration
* **Official Subtitle:** Radiative Heat Transfer, Wien’s Displacement, Planck’s Radiation Law, and Particle Scattering in Obscured Environments
* **Presenter Division:** Anju Thangachan (AI Vision & Model Specialist)
* **Four Technical Pillars on Slide:**
  1. `PLANCK'S LAW & WIEN'S LAW`: Peak human thermal emission at $9.34\ \mu\text{m}$ ($T = 310.15\ \text{K}$).
  2. `RAYLEIGH SCATTERING LAW`: Scattering intensity $I \propto \lambda^{-4}$ proves LWIR penetrates dust.
  3. `LWIR SPECTRAL WINDOW`: 8 to 14 µm transmission band avoids atmospheric moisture absorption.
  4. `THERMAL CONTRAST (HEURISTIC)`: $\Delta T = T_{\text{human}} - T_{\text{debris}} \approx 10\ \text{K}$ creates sharp detectable edges.

---

### 2. Ekdum Aasan Hinglish Samjhaav & Desi Analogy
Dekho dosto, is slide mein hum Physics ke zariye prove karte hain ki thermal camera andhere aur dhool mein kyun kaam karta hai jabki aam camera fail ho jata hai:

1. **Har Cheez Garmi Chhodti Hai (Everything Radiates):**
   Physics ka niyam hai ki universe mein jo bhi cheez Absolute Zero ($-273^\circ\text{C}$) se upar hai, woh infrared light fekti hai. Insaan ka sharir lagbhag $37^\circ\text{C}$ (yaani $310.15\ \text{Kelvin}$) par hota hai. Is temperature par insaan ke shareer se nikalne wali heat ki wave-length hoti hai **$9.34\ \mu\text{m}$** (micrometers). 
2. **Rayleigh Scattering (Dhool Se Roshni Kyun Rukti Hai?):**
   Aapne socha hai aasmaan neela kyun hota hai? Kyunki neeli roshni ki wavelength choti hoti hai toh woh hawa ke particles se takra kar phail jaati hai. 
   Lord Rayleigh ka formula kehta hai: **Scattering $\propto \frac{1}{\lambda^4}$** (Scattering wavelength ki 4th power ke inverse hoti hai!).
   * Visible light ki wavelength bohot choti hai ($0.5\ \mu\text{m}$).
   * Thermal infrared ki wavelength bohot badi hai ($10\ \mu\text{m}$, lagbhag 20 guna badi!).
   * Jab hum $20^4$ calculate karte hain: $20 \times 20 \times 20 \times 20 = 160,000$ guna kam scattering! Iska matlab thermal infrared waves building ke gire hue cement aur dhool ke kanon ke beech se bina takraye seedha nikal jaati hain!

---

### 3. Core Physics Formulas Explained

#### Formula 1: Wien's Displacement Law
$$\lambda_{\text{peak}} = \frac{b}{T} = \frac{2897.8\ \mu\text{m}\cdot\text{K}}{310.15\ \text{K}} \approx 9.34\ \mu\text{m}$$
* **Hinglish Matlab:** Kisi bhi object ka temperature jitna zyada hoga, uski heat wave utni choti hogi. Insaan ke normal body temperature ($37^\circ\text{C} = 310.15\ \text{K}$) par sabse zyada heat radiation $9.34\ \mu\text{m}$ wavelength par nikalta hai. Yeh wavelength exact LWIR window ($8-14\ \mu\text{m}$) ke beech aati hai!

#### Formula 2: Planck's Spectral Radiance Law
$$B(\lambda, T) = \frac{2hc^2}{\lambda^5} \cdot \frac{1}{e^{\frac{hc}{\lambda k_B T}} - 1}$$
* **Hinglish Matlab:** Yeh formula batata hai ki kisi specific temperature $T$ par har wavelength $\lambda$ se kitni electromagnetic energy nikal rahi hai. 

#### Formula 3: Rayleigh Scattering Cross-Section
$$\sigma_s = \frac{2\pi^5}{3} \frac{d^6}{\lambda^4} \left(\frac{n^2 - 1}{n^2 + 2}\right)^2 \implies I_s \propto \frac{1}{\lambda^4}$$
* **Hinglish Matlab:** Scattering wavelength ki 4th power se divide hoti hai. Kyunki thermal infrared ki wavelength visible light se 20 guna badi hai, iska scattering 160,000 times kam hota hai! Isliye camera dhool ke paar dekh leta hai.

---

#### Q1: "Derive or explain why Wien's displacement peak of the human body dictates your choice of sensor."

This peak emission lands squarely in the Long-Wave Infrared (LWIR) atmospheric transmission window (8 to 14 µm). Consequently, uncooled vanadium oxide (VOx) microbolometers tuned to 8–14 µm maximize signal-to-noise ratio while rejecting solar reflection."
* **Hinglish Defense:** *"Mam, Wien's law se pata chalta hai ki human body $9.34\ \mu\text{m}$ par peak heat emit karti hai. Humne LWIR sensor isliye chuna kyunki yeh sensor $8-14\ \mu\text{m}$ ke beech sensitive hota hai."*

The sensor detects surface temperature anomalies and heat escaping through voids, vents, and air pockets in the rubble pile. If a victim's metabolic heat conducts through porous debris creating a localized thermal footprint $\Delta T \ge 0.5\ \text{K}$, the sensor captures it."

### 3. Core Backend Rationale & Code Mechanics

#### A. In-Memory Video Processing (Kyun Disk Par Save Nahi Kiya?)
Agar 30 FPS video ke har frame ko disk par write kiya jaye:
$$30\ \text{frames/sec} \times 640 \times 640 \times 3\ \text{bytes} \approx 36.86\ \text{MB/sec} \implies \approx 2.2\ \text{GB/minute}!$$
Field laptop ki SSD 10 minute mein bhar jayegi aur disk read/write bottleneck ki wajah se FPS 5 par gir jayega! Isliye hum `cv2.imencode('.jpg', frame)` karke RAM buffer mein hi JPEG stream banate hain.

#### B. SQLite WAL Mode Configuration
```python
import sqlite3

def get_db_connection():
    conn = sqlite3.connect("census.db", timeout=10.0)
    # WAL mode enables concurrent readers while a writer commits
    conn.execute("PRAGMA journal_mode=WAL;")
    conn.execute("PRAGMA synchronous=NORMAL;")
    conn.row_factory = sqlite3.Row
    return conn
```
* **Kyun zaroori hai?** Standard SQLite poori database file ko lock kar deta hai write ke dauran. WAL mode mein ek separate `.wal` file banti hai jisme naye updates append hote hain, jisse readers aur writers ek doosre ko block nahi karte!

### 3. Core Annotation Mechanics & YOLO Format
YOLO format mein har photo ke saath ek `.txt` file hoti hai. Har line mein 5 numbers hote hain:
$$\text{Line Format: } \langle\text{class\_id}\rangle\quad \langle x_{\text{center}}\rangle\quad \langle y_{\text{center}}\rangle\quad \langle\text{width}\rangle\quad \langle\text{height}\rangle$$
* Saare coordinates $[0.0, 1.0]$ ke beech normalize hote hain:
  $$x_{\text{center}} = \frac{X_{\text{pixel}}}{W_{\text{image}}}, \quad y_{\text{center}} = \frac{Y_{\text{pixel}}}{H_{\text{image}}}, \quad w = \frac{W_{\text{box}}}{W_{\text{image}}}, \quad h = \frac{H_{\text{box}}}{H_{\text{image}}}$$
* **Normalize kyun karte hain?** Taaki agar image ka size change ho (jaise 1920x1080 se 640x640), toh bounding box ke mathematical coordinates hamesha accurate rahein!

---

### 4. Technical Terms - Aasan Bhasha Mein
* **FLIR Dataset:** Forward-Looking Infrared camera dwara li gayi thermal photos ka ek internationally recognized research dataset.
* **Coordinate Normalization:** Image ke pixel coordinates ko image ki total width aur height se divide karke 0 aur 1 ke beech badalna.
* **False Positive (Type I Error):** Jab malbe mein koi insaan na ho (sirf garam heater ya pipe ho), lekin AI galat alert baja de ki wahan insaan phasa hai.
* **Background Regularization:** AI model ko khali photos train karwana taaki woh false positives dena band kare.

A 70% train split allows weight updates via gradient descent; the 20% validation set enables early stopping and learning rate scheduling without data leakage; and the strictly isolated 10% test holdout guarantees an unbiased statistical measure of generalized performance on unseen disaster rubble."

### 2. Ekdum Aasan Hinglish Samjhaav & Desi Analogy
Dekho dosto, thermal cameras ki ek badi problem hoti hai: unka contrast kabhi-kabhi bohot dull (pheeka) hota hai. Agar building mein har cheez $28^\circ\text{C}$ par hai aur insaan $32^\circ\text{C}$ par hai, toh photo mein insaan aur deewar ke beech bohot halka sa farq dikhega.

Is problem ko solve karne ke liye humne 3 powerful techniques use ki:
1. **CLAHE (Contrast Limited Adaptive Histogram Equalization):**
   Aam photo editors poori photo ka contrast ek saath badha dete hain, jisse photo kharab ho jaati hai. CLAHE photo ko chhote-chhote 8x8 blocks (tiles) mein baant-ta hai aur har block ka contrast alag-alag enhance karta hai. Saath hi yeh noise ko rokta hai. Isse malbe ke peeche chhupe insaan ke shareer ke borders crystal clear dikhne lagte hain!
2. **Letterbox Resizing (Insaan Ko Khincho Mat!):**
   Camera ki photo hoti hai 16:9 ratio (rectangle) mein, lekin AI model 640x640 (square) maangta hai. Agar aap rectangle photo ko zabardasti square mein dabayenge, toh lamba insaan mota ya chipta dikhega, jisse AI confuse ho jayega! **Letterbox** technique photo ko bilkul stretch nahi karti—yeh photo ko scale karti hai aur upar-neeche grey border (padding) laga deti hai. Insaan ka shape 100% natural rehta hai!
3. **Mosaic Augmentation (4 Photos Ka Collage):**

Isse real disaster mein model partially buried victims ko bhi dhoond leta hai!

### 3. Core Preprocessing Formulas & Code

#### CLAHE Mathematical Transformation
Global Histogram Equalization poori image par $s_k = T(r_k) = \sum_{j=0}^k \frac{n_j}{N}$ lagata hai, jisse flat areas mein noise amplify ho jaata hai.
CLAHE har tile mein local histogram banata hai aur use ek Clip Limit $\beta$ par clip karta hai:
$$\beta = \frac{N}{M} \left(1 + \frac{\alpha}{100}(S_{\text{max}} - 1)\right)$$
Jo pixels clip limit ke upar nikalte hain, unhe baaki saare histogram bins mein barabar distribute (redistribute) kar diya jata hai. Iske baad bilinear interpolation lagakar tiles ke borders smooth kar diye jaate hain!

```python
import cv2

def apply_clahe_preprocessing(bgr_frame):
    # Convert BGR to YUV / LAB
    lab = cv2.cvtColor(bgr_frame, cv2.COLOR_BGR2LAB)
    l, a, b = cv2.split(lab)
    
    # Apply CLAHE to L-channel (Luminance / Thermal Intensity)
    clahe = cv2.createCLAHE(clipLimit=2.0, tileGridSize=(8, 8))
    cl = clahe.apply(l)
    
    # Merge back and convert to BGR
    limg = cv2.merge((cl, a, b))
    enhanced = cv2.cvtColor(limg, cv2.COLOR_LAB2BGR)
    return enhanced
```

## Chapter 12: Mathematical Formulations & Objective Loss Functions (Slide 12)

### 1. Slide Metadata & Visual Identity
* **Slide Category:** Loss Formulations & Mathematical Optimization
* **Official Title:** Mathematical Formulations & Objective Loss Functions
* **Official Subtitle:** Multi-Task Loss Convergence: Complete Intersection-over-Union (CIoU), Distribution Focal Loss (DFL), and Binary Cross-Entropy (BCE)
* **Presenter Division:** Anju Thangachan (AI Vision & Model Specialist)
* **Three Loss Components on Slide:**
  1. `COMPLETE IoU LOSS (CIoU)`: Penalizes overlap error ($1 - \text{IoU}$), normalized center distance ($\rho^2/c^2$), and aspect ratio inconsistency ($\alpha v$).
  2. `DISTRIBUTION FOCAL LOSS (DFL)`: Treats bounding box edges as general probability distributions over discrete bins.
  3. `BINARY CROSS-ENTROPY (BCE)`: Computes classification confidence with focal modulation for hard example mining.

---

### 2. Ekdum Aasan Hinglish Samjhaav & Desi Analogy
Dekho dosto, jab AI model train hota hai, toh woh shuru mein bohot galat predictions karta hai. Loss function woh **"Scorecard / Jurmana (Penalty)"** hai jo model ko batata hai ki usne kitni badi galti ki, taaki woh agli baar apne weights ko theek kar sake!

YOLO11n teen alag-alag galtiyon par jurmana lagata hai:
$$\mathcal{L}_{\text{total}} = \lambda_{\text{box}} \mathcal{L}_{\text{CIoU}} + \lambda_{\text{dfl}} \mathcal{L}_{\text{DFL}} + \lambda_{\text{cls}} \mathcal{L}_{\text{BCE}}$$

1. **Galti 1: Box Galat Banaya (CIoU Loss):**
   * Agar box insaan ke upar nahi hai, jurmana lagega ($1 - \text{IoU}$).
   * Agar box ka center insaan ke center se door hai, jurmana lagega ($\rho^2 / c^2$).
   * Agar box ki lambai aur chaudai ka ratio galat hai, jurmana lagega ($\alpha v$).
   In teeno ko milakar banta hai **Complete IoU (CIoU)**.
2. **Galti 2: Deewar aur Insaan Ke Beech Border Dhundhla Hai (DFL Loss):**
   Rubble mein kabhi-kabhi deewar aur insaan ka border ekdum sharp nahi hota. **Distribution Focal Loss (DFL)** box ke kinaaron ko ek single rigid number manne ke bajaye probability curve ki tarah dekhta hai taaki boundary exact fit ho!
3. **Galti 3: Insaan Ko Pathar Samajh Liya (BCE Loss):**
   Yeh classification penalty hai. Agar malbe mein insaan tha aur model ne bola *"Background"*, toh model par heavy penalty lagti hai!

---

### 3. Rigorous Mathematical Formulations

#### 1. Complete Intersection-over-Union (CIoU) Loss
$$\mathcal{L}_{\text{CIoU}} = 1 - \text{IoU} + \frac{\rho^2(b, b^{gt})}{c^2} + \alpha v$$
Where:
* $\text{IoU} = \frac{|b \cap b^{gt}|}{|b \cup b^{gt}|}$ (Predicted box $b$ aur Ground Truth box $b^{gt}$ ka overlap).
* $\rho(b, b^{gt})$ is the Euclidean distance between the center points of $b$ and $b^{gt}$.
* $c$ is the diagonal length of the smallest enclosing box covering both $b$ and $b^{gt}$.
* $v = \frac{4}{\pi^2} \left( \arctan\frac{w^{gt}}{h^{gt}} - \arctan\frac{w}{h} \right)^2$ (Aspect ratio similarity metric).
* $\alpha = \frac{v}{(1 - \text{IoU}) + v}$ (Dynamic trade-off parameter giving higher weight to aspect ratio when IoU is high).

#### 2. Distribution Focal Loss (DFL)
$$\mathcal{L}_{\text{DFL}}(S_i, S_{i+1}) = - \Big( (y_{i+1} - y) \log(S_i) + (y - y_i) \log(S_{i+1}) \Big)$$
Continuous coordinate $y$ ko do nearest discrete bins $y_i$ aur $y_{i+1}$ ke beech Softmax probabilities $S_i, S_{i+1}$ se model kiya jaata hai.

#### 3. Binary Cross-Entropy (BCE) Classification Loss
$$\mathcal{L}_{\text{BCE}} = - \Big( y \log(\hat{p}) + (1 - y) \log(1 - \hat{p}) \Big)$$

---

### 4. Technical Terms - Aasan Bhasha Mein
* **IoU (Intersection over Union):** Dono boxes ka common area divided by dono boxes ka total area. Value 0 se 1 ke beech hoti hai (1 = Perfect Match).
* **Euclidean Distance ($\rho$):** Do points $(x_1, y_1)$ aur $(x_2, y_2)$ ke beech ki seedhi geometrical doori: $\sqrt{(x_2-x_1)^2 + (y_2-y_1)^2}$.
* **Gradient Descent:** Optimization algorithm jo loss ko zero karne ke liye neural network ke weights ko dheere-dheere adjust karta hai.

---

### 5. Advanced Viva Voce Questions & Answers

#### Q1: "Why is CIoU superior to standard IoU and GIoU for bounding box regression?"
* **Model English Answer:**
  > "Standard IoU loss outputs zero gradient whenever the predicted box and ground truth box have zero intersection ($|b \cap b^{gt}| = 0$), causing training to stall. Generalized IoU (GIoU) introduced an enclosing box penalty, but it converges very slowly when one box is completely inside another. CIoU simultaneously resolves all three geometric factors: overlap area ($1-\text{IoU}$), normalized Euclidean distance between center points ($\rho^2/c^2$), and aspect ratio consistency ($\alpha v$), leading to significantly faster gradient convergence and superior localization precision."
* **Hinglish Defense:** *"Mam, aam IoU tab fail ho jata hai jab boxes touch nahi karte kyunki gradient zero ho jata hai. GIoU slow hai. CIoU overlap, center distance aur aspect ratio teeno ko ek saath penalize karta hai, isliye model bohot jaldi accurate box banana seekh jaata hai."*

#### Q2: "What is the physical meaning of the $\alpha$ parameter in the CIoU formula?"
* **Model English Answer:**
  > "The parameter $\alpha = v / ((1 - \text{IoU}) + v)$ acts as an adaptive weighting mechanism. When the predicted box and ground-truth box have low overlap (low IoU), $(1 - \text{IoU})$ dominates the denominator, making $\alpha$ small and prioritizing overlap area and center alignment over aspect ratio. Once the boxes overlap significantly (high IoU), $(1 - \text{IoU})$ approaches zero, driving $\alpha \to 1$, which forces fine-tuning of the aspect ratio $w/h$."

### 3. Core Mathematical Formulations

#### 1. Cosine Annealing Learning Rate Decay
$$\eta_t = \eta_{\text{min}} + \frac{1}{2}(\eta_{\text{max}} - \eta_{\text{min}})\left(1 + \cos\left(\frac{T_{\text{cur}}}{T_{\text{max}}}\pi\right)\right)$$
* Shuruat mein $T_{\text{cur}} = 0 \implies \cos(0) = 1 \implies \eta_t = \eta_{\text{max}} = 0.01$.
* Aakhir mein $T_{\text{cur}} = T_{\text{max}} \implies \cos(\pi) = -1 \implies \eta_t = \eta_{\text{min}} = 0.0001$.
* Is smooth curve se loss function bina kisi jhatke (oscillation) ke smoothly minimum par pahunch jata hai!

#### 2. Momentum Update Formula
$$v_t = \beta \cdot v_{t-1} + \eta_t \cdot \nabla_\theta \mathcal{L}(\theta_t)$$
$$\theta_{t+1} = \theta_t - v_t$$

#### 1. Precision & Recall Formulas
$$\text{Precision} = \frac{\text{TP}}{\text{TP} + \text{FP}} = \frac{142}{142 + 21} = \frac{142}{163} \approx 0.798\ (79.8\%)$$
$$\text{Recall} = \frac{\text{TP}}{\text{TP} + \text{FN}} = \frac{142}{142 + 26} = \frac{142}{168} \approx 0.745\ (74.5\%)$$

#### 2. F1-Score (Harmonic Mean)
$$F_1 = 2 \cdot \frac{\text{Precision} \cdot \text{Recall}}{\text{Precision} + \text{Recall}} = 2 \cdot \frac{0.798 \times 0.745}{0.798 + 0.745} \approx 0.770$$

#### 3. Mean Average Precision (mAP@0.50)
$$\text{AP} = \int_0^1 p(r) \, dr \implies \text{mAP} = \frac{1}{N_{\text{classes}}} \sum_{k=1}^{N_{\text{classes}}} \text{AP}_k = 81.2\%$$

---

### 4. Technical Terms - Aasan Bhasha Mein
* **True Positive (TP):** Malbe mein zinda insaan tha aur AI ne sahi jagah red box banaya (Bilkul Sahi!).
* **False Positive (FP):** Malbe mein sirf garam heater ya pipe tha, lekin AI ne bola insaan hai (Jhoota Alert).
* **False Negative (FN):** Malbe mein sach mein insaan phasa tha, lekin AI use dekh nahi paya (Sabse Khatarnak Galti).
* **mAP@0.50:0.95:** Bounding box ki accuracy ko 10 alag-alag strict levels (50% match se lekar 95% perfect match) par check karke unka average nikalna.

#### Q1: "In search-and-rescue, which metric is more critical: Precision or Recall? Why?"
* **Model English Answer:**
  > "In Urban Search and Rescue (USAR), **Recall** is life-critical and takes precedence over Precision. A false positive (low precision) merely wastes 5 to 10 minutes of rescue inspection time. However, a false negative (low recall) means a trapped living victim is completely missed, resulting in certain death under the 72-hour survival decay.
